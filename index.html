Leave the SIM where it is. You stay overseas. Send and receive that number’s SMS from your email.

  1. 1. Which phone

    Install the app on the leftover Android phone that still has the SIM. Sign in with the same email you use every day, then turn on Gateway. That phone must stay on and able to send and receive SMS. Your overseas phone does not need the app.

  2. 2. Incoming SMS

    Texts that arrive on the SIM are forwarded to your inbox as email. Read them there — you don’t need to operate the leftover phone remotely.

  3. 3. Send a new SMS

    Compose a new email to:

    +[country code][number]@sms.airppp.com

    The plus sign is required. For the US use +1…@sms.airppp.com; for China, +86…@sms.airppp.com. Put the SMS text in the body; the subject works too.

  4. 4. Reply to an SMS

    Don’t start a new email. Reply to the notification that someone texted you. Your reply is sent back as SMS.
